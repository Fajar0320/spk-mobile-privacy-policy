Privacy Policy — SPK Mobile

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the SPK Mobile app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, operated by PT Great Giant Pineapple on behalf of the Great Giant Foods group (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). The Application is an internal work tool used by employees of PT Great Giant Pineapple and PT NSA, members of the Great Giant Foods group, to manage plantation Work Orders (Surat Perintah Kerja / SPK). Only employees who have been given an account by their company can use the Application. This service is provided "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you use it. This information may include:

  • Account information: name, employee ID / user ID, email, job role (Mandor, Kasie, Kabag, Admin), and work unit.
  • Work data: Work Orders (SPK), daily activity and result reports (LKH, LHO, LHR, LKB), approvals, and revision history.
  • Workforce attendance recorded through the Application.
  • Photos and attachments you take with the camera or select from your gallery as proof of work.
  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (IP address), operating system, device model, and Application version.
  • Notification token and error logs, used to deliver notifications and fix problems.

The Application collects your device's location to record where work is carried out in the plantation, when you use a feature that requires location. Location data is used only for operational purposes of the Service Provider and is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

The Application requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera: to take photos as proof of work.
  • Location: to record your position in the plantation when a related feature is used.
  • Storage / gallery: to select photos or files as attachments.
  • Notifications: to inform you of new Work Orders, approval requests, or revisions.

The Application does not collect your contacts, SMS, or call history.

The information collected is used to run the Work Order process (create, fill in, approve, and report plantation work), to send work realization to the company's internal systems (SAP) and attendance system, to keep accounts secure, and to improve the Application. The Service Provider may use your information to contact you with important information and required notices related to your work. The information will not be used for marketing or advertising.

Offline Storage

So that it can be used in the field without signal, some data is stored temporarily on your device and sent to the server when the device is connected to the internet again.

Third Party Access

Your information is accessed only by authorized employees of PT Great Giant Pineapple and PT NSA according to their role, and by trusted service providers who work on behalf of the Service Provider (including cloud hosting by Amazon Web Services and the Application's development vendor). These providers do not have an independent use of the information and have agreed to keep it confidential.

The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with their trusted service providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information disclosed to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

Opt-Out Rights

You can turn off camera, location, storage, or notification permissions at any time through your device settings, although some features may not work without them. You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it, using the standard uninstall process of your mobile device or the application marketplace.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain work data for as long as it is needed for the company's operations and audit, and in accordance with applicable laws. Accounts are deactivated when the user is no longer employed by PT Great Giant Pineapple or PT NSA. In accordance with Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting Innovation.dept@gg-foods.com, and the Service Provider will respond in a reasonable time.

Children

The Application is intended for employees only and does not address anyone under the age of 18. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If the Service Provider discovers that a child has provided personal information, it will be deleted immediately from their servers.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), access to the Application requires a company-issued account, and the Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information it processes and maintains.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-11-06.
